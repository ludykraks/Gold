It's been a while since we saw any new innovation in mobile money transfers, but that ends today. MTN Ghana has now introduced money transfer across the borders of Ghana.

And it is very easy and seamless. You can now transfer money from your MTN Mobile Money (MTN MoMo) account to another MTN account in Nigeria.


## Can I mobile money to Nigeria?
Yes, you can send cash from your MTN mobile money wallet to a MTN account in Nigeria. All you need is money in your wallet and the receiver's phone number

And it is instant.

## How to Send Monday from MTN Ghana to Nigeria
1. Dial the MTN Ghana Mobile Money short code *170#
2. Select Transfer Money
3. Choose Cross Border Payment
4. Select Transfer to Wallet
5. And select the country e.g Nigeria
6. And enter the receiver's phone number
7. Confirm the phone number
8. Type the Amount
9. Enter Reference 
10. And enter your PIN

That's all folks, money has been sent. You will receive an SMS to confirm the transaction.
