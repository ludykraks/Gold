Document provider contracts, access controls, subprocessors and deletion handling. Add any material recipient information to the notice.
2. Agree a retention schedule and implement it across Netlify, email, backups and any CRM. Suggested starting point for approval: review unconverted enquiries and inactive leads after six months, delete at twelve months unless an active discussion or documented legal reason justifies retention. These are proposals, not existing practices or legal deadlines. Keep a minimal suppression list so deleted prospects are not contacted again. Confirm applicable accounting/claims retention separately.
3. Check the actual Netlify and email account processing agreements and data locations. Determine which flows are restricted transfers, including onward disclosures, and record the applicable EU SCCs, UK Addendum/IDTA or other valid mechanism, with transfer risk assessment and supplementary measures where needed. Direct disclosure by an individual to Ghana is not automatically the same as a controller-to-controller restricted transfer. Replace the draft transfer paragraph with the verified mechanisms and how to obtain copies.
4. Assess EU GDPR and UK GDPR territorial scope for the planned targeted offering. Assess each regime's representative requirement separately and add appointed representatives' contact details if required. Do not assume that being a small Ghana-based business creates an exemption. Check Ghana Act 843 registration and other applicable obligations; assess any UK registration/fee obligation. Assess whether a DPO is required; do not invent a DPO or representative.
5. Test the owner's certified AdSense CMP on the actual ad-serving domain with UK/EEA settings, including reject, granular choice and withdrawal/reopening. Audit cookies and other storage, their purposes, vendors and lifetimes; expose those details in the CMP/cookie notice. No AdSense script or CMP was found in the checked development templates, so an account setting alone has not been independently verified. Do not add a generic homemade banner in place of Google's certified CMP. Analytics/Google Ads remain future options and require a fresh assessment before activation.
6. Older articles have directly loaded YouTube and ArcGIS iframes. Audit their storage/network behaviour. Gate non-essential embedded content behind informed consent or replace it with external links before claiming consent-compliant operation. Do not describe lazy loading or youtube-nocookie alone as consent. No embed behaviour was changed in this privacy-page PR.
7. Verify all factual and operational statements, including request handling. Arrange a focused legal review for the actual target countries and channels. This notice cannot make an otherwise prohibited campaign lawful.

## Outreach process

- Record lead source/date, country, business type, relevant role, proposed channel, lawful basis and any consent evidence. Collect only the professional details needed. Avoid inferring sensitive traits or buying/scraping lists without a separate assessment.
- Document a legitimate-interests assessment where relying on that basis. Public contact details do not imply consent. UK corporate-email rules are different from those for sole traders/some partnerships. A privacy link is not a substitute for consent where required.
- For each EU destination, check its national electronic-marketing rules before sending. Do not apply the UK's corporate-email exemption throughout the EU. Consent-first is the conservative default until the country/channel assessment is complete.
- For UK marketing calls, screen against TPS/CTPS and your own suppression list; identify the caller and display a valid number. Check destination-specific EU do-not-call/consent requirements. Automated calls need a separate assessment and generally prior consent.
- At first outreach, identify Goldchest, explain why the message is relevant and where the contact information came from, link the final privacy notice and give a simple free opt-out. Supply Article 14 information within the applicable deadline, not just when someone asks.
- Apply opt-outs across every tool and channel covered by the objection. Keep minimal suppression data. Do not add enquiry submitters to marketing lists automatically. Disable email open/click tracking unless separately assessed and lawfully configured.
- Keep a request/complaint process and breach-response procedure. Where required, regulator breach notification can have a 72-hour deadline; assess notification to affected people separately. Client work involving customer data needs suitable processor terms, access restrictions and instructions.
