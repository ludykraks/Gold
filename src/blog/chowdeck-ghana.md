<p>Ghana's food delivery scene is heating up. Bolt Food has been operating in Ghana for years, but now <strong>ChowDeck</strong> is here to serve competition, convenience and creativity right to your doorstep.</p>
<p> Whether you're craving crispy kelewele or tower burgers, ChowDeck lets you explore Accra's food culture from the comfort of your screen.</p>

<p>Unlike Bolt, originally a taxi company turned food courier, ChowDeck was built with the sole mission of revolutionizing food delivery. No distractions. Just good food, fast.</p>

<h2>How to Order from ChowDeck</h2>

<p>Getting started is easy. Here's your step-by-step guide:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Download the ChowDeck app from your app store</li>
  <li>Sign up and set your delivery location</li>
  <li>Browse restaurants and meals curated for every taste</li>
  <li>Place your order, pay securely, and wait for your rider</li>
  <li>Enjoy hot food delivered to your door</li>
</ul>

<h2>Delivery Areas</h2>
<p>ChowDeck currently delivers to many of the same high-demand neighborhoods as Bolt Food, including Osu, East Legon, Circle, Kanda, Ring Road, Airport, 37 and Ayawaso districts. And yes, they’re expanding fast.</p>

<h2>Promos Worth Enjoying</h2>
<p>New users enjoy <strong>up to 30% Discounts</strong>. Plus, watch out for regular flash discounts from partner restaurants including Papa's Pizza, KFC Ghana, Cheezzy Pizza 🍕 and more.</p>
