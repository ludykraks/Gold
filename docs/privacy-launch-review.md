- Zoho Mail is the current business email provider. Netlify hosts the website and handles enquiry forms. Google AdSense is enabled; the owner confirms a certified UK/EU consent message is enabled.

## Simple operating routine

Review enquiry and lead records monthly. Apply the agreed cut-offs to Netlify Forms, Zoho Mail and any exported lists. Check spam/trash and provider backup/deletion rules. Do not delete records subject to a specific legal hold or required retention obligation; document the reason and restrict their use. No existing personal records were deleted by this code change.

For every outreach record, keep the source, date, business type, country and contact/opt-out status. Identify Goldchest, explain the relevance and source at first contact, link the privacy notice and provide an easy opt-out. Check the recipient country's email/call rules; public availability is not consent. Screen applicable telephone preference registers before calls. Do not automatically subscribe enquiry submitters to marketing.

## Provider account checks

Netlify's published DPA forms part of its service agreement and includes EU SCCs and the UK transfer addendum for covered transfers. Zoho offers a separate DPA via Zoho Accounts > Settings > Privacy > Data Processing Addendum. The account administrator should check whether it is signed, complete it if required, and retain a copy. Confirm the Zoho account region from the account/domain settings and keep that record. No DPA was signed or accepted on the owner's behalf, and the account region has not been independently confirmed. Assess any applicable restricted transfer and additional safeguards; published provider terms alone do not prove every account arrangement is complete.

The owner confirms an existing certified AdSense consent message. Its regional reject/withdraw/reopen behaviour was not independently tested; no AdSense/CMP scripts were found in the checked development templates. Check it on the actual ad-serving domain before new UK/EEA traffic campaigns. A custom generic banner is not a replacement for Google's certified CMP.

Assess EU/UK territorial scope and representative requirements for the actual targeted offering, as well as Ghana registration obligations. A privacy notice does not itself authorise a marketing campaign.
