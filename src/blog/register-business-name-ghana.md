Registering a business name is one of the first formal steps you can take when starting a small business in Ghana.

The Office of the Registrar of Companies (ORC) refers to this type of registration as a **Business Name** or **Sole proprietorship**. It is designed for a business owned by one person.

This simple guide will show you how to register, how much it costs and what you should do after receiving your certificate.

> **Note:** The standard processing fee for registering a business name as **GH₵130**. An optional VIP service costs an additional **GH₵650**. The registered name must then be renewed every year for **GH₵70**. Check the [official ORC business registration page](https://orc.gov.gh/service-business-registration.php) before paying because fees and procedures may change.

## Should I register a Sole Proprietorship Business in Ghana?

A business name is suitable when one person owns and controls the business. It is commonly used by freelancers, traders, artisans, consultants and other small business owners.

The owner receives the profits and makes the decisions, but also carries the liabilities of the business. This means a sole proprietorship does not create the same legal separation between the owner and the business that a limited liability company does.

If the business will have more than one owner, outside investors or significant financial risk, you may need to consider a partnership or company instead.

## How Much Does It Cost to Register a Business Name in Ghana?

According to the Registrar's General Department, the current fees are:

| Service | Current fee |
| --- | ---: |
| Standard business name registration | GH₵130 |
| Optional VIP service | GH₵650 in addition to the processing fee |
| Annual business name renewal | GH₵70 |

These are official fees listed by the office as of September 2026.

## Step 1: Choose Your Business Name

Prepare two or three possible names before you begin. Having different names ready will help if your first choice is unavailable or too similar to an existing name.

The ORC states that a proposed name should not be the same as, or confusingly similar in sound or appearance to, an existing business name. It should also not be misleading or undesirable.

If the name uses words from a language other than English, you may be asked to provide an interpretation.

Choose a name that is:

- Easy to spell and remember
- Relevant to the business
- Distinct from competitors
- Suitable for a website and social media accounts, you can check online if the domain exists already
- Flexible enough to remain useful if the business grows, should you want to branch out into different things


## Step 2: Search for the Name Online

Use the [ORC online name-search tool](https://rgdonline.gegov.gov.gh/orc-app/) to check whether your preferred business name is already in the database.

The search tool allows you to look for an exact match or names that start with, end with or contain particular words.

An online search is helpful, but the Registrar makes the final decision on whether a name can be approved.

## Step 3: Obtain and Complete Form A

The prescribed form for a sole proprietorship is **Form A**. Applicants may obtain it from the bank within their offices or download it when the current form is available online.

Read every section carefully and make sure the information is correct before signing it.

It helps to have your proposed business name, the nature of the business, the principal business address and the owner's details ready before completing the form.

## Step 4: Submit the Form for Verification

Submit the completed and signed Form A at the Business Names Registration Counter.

An officer will verify the information and capture the application. Check the spelling of the owner's name, the business name, address and business activity before the application proceeds.

Small errors can become inconvenient later when you need to open a business bank account, apply for permits, sign contracts or register with another institution.

## Step 5: Pay the Registration Fee

Pay the **GH₵130 processing fee** through the payment channel specified by the ORC.

If you choose the optional VIP service, it would cost you an additional fee of **GH₵650**. 

Keep your receipt and any application reference you receive.

## Step 6: Collect Your Registration Documents

After the application has been examined and approved, the ORC issues:

- A Certificate of Registration
- A Certified True Copy of Form A

Check the documents as soon as you receive them. If you notice an error, ask the ORC how to correct it before using the documents for other registrations or business transactions.

Store both digital scans and the original paper copies securely.

## What to Do After Registering the Business

Registration is an important beginning, but it is not the final step in setting up the business.

Depending on what you do and where you operate, your next steps may include:

- Registering for any applicable tax obligations
- Obtaining permits or licences required for your industry
- Opening a dedicated business bank or mobile money account
- Keeping proper records of income and expenses
- Creating business invoices and receipts
- Securing your domain name and social media usernames
- Creating a professional website and business email address

## Remember to Renew the Business Name Every Year

A registered business name must be renewed annually. The ORC currently lists the annual renewal fee as **GH₵70** and says business-name owners can use the USSD code `*222#` for renewal.

Do not wait until you urgently need proof that the business is in good standing. Set a yearly reminder and keep every renewal receipt with your registration documents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I complete the entire registration online?

The ORC provides an online name-search tool. However, its currently published business-name procedure directs applicants to complete Form A and submit it at the Business Names Registration Counter. Check the ORC website before starting because its online services are being expanded.

### How much is business name registration in Ghana in 2026?

The ORC currently lists the standard processing fee as GH₵130. The optional VIP service is GH₵650 in addition to the processing fee.

### What documents will I receive?

Once the application is approved, the ORC says it issues a Certificate of Registration and a Certified True Copy of Form A.

### Does a business name give me limited liability?

No. A business name is a sole proprietorship, and the owner bears the liabilities of the business. Consider getting professional advice if the business will carry substantial risk or have several owners.

### Must I renew the registration every year?

Yes. The ORC states that renewal is mandatory every year. The current annual renewal fee is GH₵70.

## Final Thoughts

Registering a business name in Ghana is a straightforward way for a single owner to formalise a small business.

Start by choosing a distinct name, confirm its availability, complete Form A carefully, pay the official fee and keep the documents you receive. After registration, remember to renew the name every year and build a professional presence that helps customers find and trust the business.
